About BLK-WHT

BLK-WHT represents a philosophy: clarity through contrast. Just as our 2D drawings are rendered on white panels and 3D models are presented in black for maximum clarity, we bring precision and visibility to every CAD project.

With 10+ years of experience in mechanical design and CAD conversion, I specialize in:

  • Converting legacy 2D drawings into optimized 3D models
  • Industrial equipment design and re-engineering
  • Large assembly modeling with complex documentation
  • Sheet metal and weldment design
  • Manufacturing-ready technical drawings and BOMs
  • Imperial ↔ Metric conversions

Why choose BLK-WHT?

  • ⚡ Speed: Quick turnaround times without compromising quality
  • ✅ Quality: Precision designs ready for manufacturing
  • 💰 Affordability: Competitive rates for global teams
  • 🌍 Global Ready: Experience working with international teams across time zones
  • 🤝 Professional: 10+ years of proven expertise
